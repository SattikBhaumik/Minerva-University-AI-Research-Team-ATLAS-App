# Minerva University graduate-outcome numbers on its official social media accounts

Method note (applies to every section): 16 tool calls were made (14 web searches, 2 direct fetches). Direct fetching of social platforms failed in this environment: `www.linkedin.com` and `medium.com` both returned "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND" (the hosts are outside the session's allowed network domains). Instagram, X, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok were not fetched for the same reason. Searches limited to those platforms (`site:linkedin.com`, `site:instagram.com`, handle searches for X, platform-name searches for Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Threads) returned almost no social-platform pages. The search engine returned minerva.edu pages instead. Everything below about social posts is therefore "snippet only" unless marked otherwise. No social post text was read in full.

## Which outcome numbers has Minerva posted on social media in 2024-2026, and do they match its website?

### Takeaway
I found no post from any official Minerva social account (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok) dated 2024-2026 that states an undergraduate outcome statistic. I also found no per-college figure (CS, NS, Business, Social Sciences, Arts & Humanities) on any social platform. The only outcome number traced to an official Minerva social or blog channel is an older Medium figure, "92%", from founder Ben Nelson's 2020 annual letter. I could not compare that wording against the website verbatim because medium.com could not be fetched.

### Cited Findings
- **Medium (official "Minerva University" publication, earlier "Minerva Schools"), "Annual Letter For 2020 From Our Founder" (Ben Nelson):** a search-engine summary says the letter reports "92%" of graduates employed or in further education within six months and compares that with top Ivy League outcomes. The summary gives the post date as January 24, 2020. Snippet only, not verbatim. The exact sentence, the date and the cohort could not be verified because a direct fetch of medium.com failed. — [Medium, minerva-university](https://medium.com/minerva-university/annual-letter-for-2020-from-our-founder-65a18b95125d); same post at the older publication path [Medium, minerva-schools](https://medium.com/minerva-schools/annual-letter-for-2020-from-our-founder-65a18b95125d)
- **Medium, "Annual Letter For 2019 From Our Founder":** the search excerpt held no outcome statistic. Snippet only. — [Medium](https://medium.com/minerva-university/annual-letter-for-2019-from-our-founder-e3c1c8399258)
- **Medium, "Making Healthcare Accessible with Alumna Chisom Egwuatu":** an alumni profile (Class of 2020). No aggregate outcome number appeared in the snippet. — [Medium](https://medium.com/minerva-university/making-healthcare-accessible-with-alumnus-chisom-egwuatu-b17f7ccff252)
- **LinkedIn (company page "minerva-university"):** the only post the searches surfaced was a podcast promo tagged #highereducation #artificialintelligence #futureofeducation #ai (activity 7090659573035909120). No outcome statistic appeared in its snippet. Searches for "91%", "within six months", "employed or in graduate school" and "Class of 2025" limited to linkedin.com returned no Minerva post with outcome numbers. — [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minerva-university_highereducation-artificialintelligence-futureofeducation-activity-7090659573035909120-qp1P)
- **Website comparison figures (context only; these are not social posts).** The website wording varies from page to page:
  - "91% of graduates are working or in graduate school within six months", with "97% completed at least one internship before graduation" — [Student Impact](https://www.minerva.edu/student-impact)
  - "91% Of graduates employed or in graduate school within 6 months" — [About](https://www.minerva.edu/about/)
  - "91% of graduates are employed full-time or in graduate school within 6 months of graduation" — [Undergraduate](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/)
  - "91% of graduates are in full-time roles or graduate school" — [Career support](https://www.minerva.edu/career-support)
  - The older impact page lists "15% continued with graduate school or academic fellowships" and a 12% founder figure. The extracted labels were jumbled. — [impact-outcomes-old](https://www.minerva.edu/impact-outcomes-old)
  - All of the above are search snippets, not verified page reads.
- **Third-party figures, for contrast (not official social).** Forbes (2021) reported "88%" of Minerva graduates landed jobs or graduate programs within six months. — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/nelldebevoise/2021/07/29/minerva-universitys-plan-to-out-elite-harvard/) (snippet only)

### Inferences
- The searches found no official social post repeating the website figures: neither the overall 91% nor any per-college figure (CS 95.6% of 256, NS 85% of 46, Business 94.8% of 97, Social Sciences 93%, Arts & Humanities 82% of 104). This is a search-visibility finding, not proof that no such post exists. LinkedIn and Instagram post bodies are poorly indexed and could not be fetched here.
- The Medium "92%" (2020) and the current website "91%" probably describe different cohort pools. The 2020 letter came out when only the first graduating class (2019) existed. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- Verbatim post text, dates and URLs for LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok could not be obtained. Direct fetching was blocked (`www.linkedin.com` and `medium.com` failed to resolve), and the search engine did not index matching posts. Finishing this check requires allowing those hosts in the session's network settings, or checking the accounts by hand.
- The exact wording of the 92% sentence in the 2020 Medium letter is unverified.

## Are there class-specific posts (Class of 2024, 2025, 2026 destinations, commencement posts) with numbers?

### Takeaway
I found no class-specific outcome number on any official social account. Minerva's class-destination content appears on its own website blog ("Beyond Graduation" posts for 2022-2026) and in a 2026 commencement announcement. Per the snippets, these give named destinations and a headcount but no placement percentage. Whether the social accounts reposted them could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Class of 2025 "Beyond Graduation" (website blog, May 20, 2025 per search summary):** says graduates are heading into "full-time roles at leading tech companies or pursuing graduate studies at top institutions." Named examples: Dimitar, Ph.D. in Human Evolutionary Biology at Harvard; Jiayuan (Tian), Master of International Affairs at Columbia SIPA and a Diana Award recipient; Jan Bartkowiak, Schwarzman Scholar, Global Affairs at Tsinghua. No percentage appeared in the snippet. Website, not social; snippet only. — [minerva.edu blog](https://origin-www.minerva.edu/blog/class-of-2025-beyond-graduation)
- **Class of 2026 commencement announcement (website, May 28, 2026 per search summary):** the ceremony at Herbst Theatre, San Francisco, honored "nearly 120 undergraduate and graduate students." It says "Graduates from the Class of 2026 have secured opportunities across technology, finance, sustainability and research," and names Oxford graduate study and roles at Encord, Vanguard and CDP. No percentage. Snippet only. — [minerva.edu announcement](https://webflow.minerva.edu/announcements/minerva-university-celebrates-2026-graduates-at-commencement-ceremony-in-san-francisco)
- **Class of 2026 destinations blog:** per the snippet, it mentions graduate study at Oxford, Harvard and Stanford, with no percentage. — [minerva.edu blog](https://www.minerva.edu/blog/beyond-graduation-where-the-class-of-2026-is-headed)
- **Class of 2024 and Class of 2023 "Beyond Graduation" blogs** exist. Their contents were not read. — [2024](https://minerva.edu/blog/class-of-2024-beyond-graduation); [2023](https://www.minerva.edu/blog/class-of-2023-beyond-graduation/)
- **Searches for platform posts** ("Instagram class of 2025 graduates headed", "YouTube commencement 2026 percent employed", "TikTok OR threads ... within 6 months") returned no social-platform results. — search results listed only minerva.edu and aggregator pages

### Inferences
- Minerva's class-level communication appears to rely on named examples rather than class-specific rates. Any social posts that promote these blogs would likely carry the same no-percentage format. This is unverified for the social accounts themselves.

### Gaps
- I could not find commencement posts on Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook or YouTube for 2024, 2025 or 2026, so I could not check them for numbers.

## Any posts with numbers that differ from the website, or that give newer cohorts?

### Takeaway
The only differing number found in an official Minerva channel outside the website is the Medium founder letter's "92%" (2020), set against the website's current "91%". I found no social post giving a newer cohort's rate.

### Cited Findings
- Medium 2020 founder letter: "92%" (snippet only). The website now says "91%". — [Medium](https://medium.com/minerva-university/annual-letter-for-2020-from-our-founder-65a18b95125d); [Student Impact](https://www.minerva.edu/student-impact)
- The website's own wording drifts across pages ("working or in graduate school", "employed full-time or in graduate school", "in full-time roles or graduate school"). Any social repost would need checking against the specific page it quotes. — [About](https://www.minerva.edu/about/); [Undergraduate](https://www.minerva.edu/undergraduate/); [Career support](https://www.minerva.edu/career-support)
- Base for comparison: Minerva's December 2022 Alumni Success Report (a PDF hosted by a third party, not a social post) says its data was "collected in November of 2022 for our graduating classes of 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022" and that it reflects "96% of alumni." — [Alumni Success Report PDF](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf) (snippet only)

### Inferences
- The 92% to 91% change likely reflects a larger, multi-cohort pool over time rather than a contradiction. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- I found no official social post giving a Class of 2024, 2025 or 2026 placement rate, a sample size, or per-college numbers.
- Explicit absence by platform, for the writer:
  - LinkedIn: none found
  - Instagram: none found
  - X/Twitter: none found
  - Facebook: none found
  - YouTube: none found
  - TikTok/Threads: none found
  - Medium: one older figure (92%, 2020), snippet only
