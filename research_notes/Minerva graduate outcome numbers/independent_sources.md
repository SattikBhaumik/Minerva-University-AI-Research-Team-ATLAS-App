# Independent (non-Minerva) sources on Minerva University undergraduate outcomes

Method note for the report writer: this session's network proxy blocked direct fetches of collegescorecard.ed.gov, nces.ed.gov (College Navigator / IPEDS), datausa.io, univstats.com and cdn.prod.website-files.com (which hosts the WSCUC action letter PDF). **Every figure below is "snippet only"**: it comes from web-search result text, which a model summarized, not from a page I read in full. Numbers are copied as they appeared in the snippets. Confirm them on the source page before quoting them as final. Minerva's IPEDS/Scorecard UnitID appears in aggregator URLs as **484844** (e.g. collegeraptor.com/.../Minerva-University-CA--484844, datausa.io/profile/university/484844). Searches were run 2026-11-21.

## 1. What do College Scorecard and IPEDS report for Minerva (graduation rate, retention, median earnings), and for which cohorts?

### Takeaway
Federal-data mirrors agree on a first-time, full-time 150%-time (six-year) graduation rate of **88.8% / 88.83%**, labeled "2023" by DataUSA. They also agree on full-time first-year retention of **about 98% (97.97%)**. **No median earnings figure is published for Minerva** at any horizon. Aggregators report it as "N/A" or "Not reported", most likely because federal earnings data covers only students who received federal aid, and very few Minerva students take federal loans. Other graduation-rate figures (79%, 81%, 83.13%, 86.28%, 0.0%) appear on secondary sites and use different definitions or data years.

### Inferences
- The most defensible federal figure is the IPEDS first-time, full-time 150%-time graduation rate of **88.8% (88.83%)**, which DataUSA labels "2023". IPEDS GR data labeled 2023 normally covers students who entered in fall 2017 (the second entering class, Class of 2021), but I could not confirm the cohort year on an ed.gov page.
- The 81%, 83.13% and 79% figures probably come from earlier cohorts (for example the founding fall 2014 cohort) or from a different measure. They do not contradict 88.8% so much as describe different years. Even so, they show that Minerva's completion rate has varied across cohorts.
- The lack of Scorecard earnings data means **no federal source can confirm or refute** Minerva's employment and salary claims.

### Gaps

## 2. What placement numbers have journalists reported, and where did they get them?

### Takeaway
Independent journalism with outcome numbers is sparse, and every number found traces back to Minerva. Forbes (2021) reported **88%** in jobs or graduate school within six months. Times Higher Education (2022) reported Minerva *claims* a graduation rate "near 85 per cent". The 2024 Daedalus article that reports outcomes is **written by Minerva insiders**, so it is not independent. Searches of Inside Higher Ed, EdSurge, The Atlantic, the NYT and Bloomberg returned no articles with Minerva placement numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **Forbes, 29 July 2021** (Nell DeBevoise, "Minerva University's Plan To Out-Elite Harvard"): "88% of Minerva graduates have landed full-time professional jobs or entered highly respected graduate programs within six months of graduation." Snippet only. The source is not named in the snippet, but the article predates any federal outcome data, so the figure is almost certainly Minerva-supplied. — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/nelldebevoise/2021/07/29/minerva-universitys-plan-to-out-elite-harvard/)
- **Times Higher Education, 2022** ("Minerva comes of age, hoping it's not too late"): Minerva "claims a graduation rate near 85 per cent". The piece describes Minerva as generating "off-the-charts rates of admissions selectivity, social diversity and professional outcomes", which the search summary says relies largely on Minerva's own account. Snippet only; the result came from a THE staging host. — [THE (staging mirror)](https://d7.stg.timeshighereducation.com/node/722015)
- **Daedalus, Spring 2024** ("Minerva: The Intentional University", American Academy of Arts & Sciences): "15 percent have been admitted to prestigious graduate and professional schools; another 15 percent started their own ventures; and the rest are employed in a variety of jobs related to their studies"; it puts retention and graduation at "about 90 percent". **Not independent:** the authors are Teri A. Cannon (Interim and Founding President of Minerva University, 2019–2023) and Stephen M. Kosslyn (Minerva's founding Chief Academic Officer). Snippet only. — [MIT Press Daedalus](https://direct.mit.edu/daed/article/153/2/275/121282/Minerva-The-Intentional-University); [AmAcad PDF](https://amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/Daedalus_Sp24_22_Cannon%20%26%20Kosslyn.pdf)
- **Charles Koch Foundation, July 2019** (a funder-published piece, not journalism) said "many students in the first graduating undergraduate class are beginning their careers at prominent organizations or furthering their education". No number is given. — [Charles Koch Foundation](https://charleskochfoundation.org/?p=2372)
- **Niche** (college guide): "91% employed 1 year after graduation" and "89% employed 5 years after graduation". Snippet only. Niche's after-college pages usually draw employment figures from federal (Dept. of Education / Treasury) data, but the snippet does not name a source, and Minerva has no federal earnings record (section 1). **Treat as low reliability until the source line is checked.** The 91% exactly matches Minerva's own headline. — [Niche after-college](https://www.niche.com/colleges/minerva-university/after-college/)
- **AcademicJobs.com**: "91% of the Class of 2023 were employed or pursuing graduate studies within six months", citing only "Minerva's official career outcomes". **Low reliability**; this is a repackaged Minerva figure. — [AcademicJobs acceptance-rate page](https://www.academicjobs.com/employers/minerva-university/4789/acceptance-rate)
- **MyVisaJobs** (independent records of US green-card / LCA sponsorship): lists "Google, Jobs Sponsored: 2" as the top sponsoring employer of Minerva graduates, with other employers at one each, over about 8 years. This is a narrow slice (sponsorship filings only), not a placement rate. Snippet only. — [MyVisaJobs](https://www.myvisajobs.com/university/minerva-university)

### Inferences
- Every placement percentage found in the press (88% in 2021; 91% from 2022 onward) matches Minerva's own figures for the same period. I found **no journalist who independently surveyed graduates or used non-Minerva data**.
- The jump from 88% (Forbes 2021) to 91% (later Minerva materials) is consistent with Minerva updating its own survey, not with an independent measurement.

### Gaps
- No Inside Higher Ed, EdSurge, Atlantic, NYT or Bloomberg article with Minerva graduate placement numbers turned up in the searches. Older pieces, such as The Atlantic's 2014 "The Future of College", predate Minerva's first graduates (2019). I could not read the full THE 2022 or Forbes 2021 articles to check their attributions verbatim.

## 3. Do any independent sources break outcomes down by major or college?

### Takeaway
No independent source gives **employment or earnings outcomes by college**. The only independent per-field data is IPEDS degree **completions** by CIP code, via DataUSA. Those counts are much smaller than Minerva's per-college survey denominators, because the two count different things: one year's degrees versus several cohorts of graduates. IPEDS also does not map cleanly onto Minerva's five colleges.

### Cited Findings
- DataUSA, IPEDS completions for 2024: "155 degrees were awarded across all undergraduate and graduate programs". "The most common Bachelor's Degree concentration at Minerva University is Mathematics & Computer Science (57 degrees awarded), followed by General Business Administration & Management (35 degrees) and Humanistic Studies (22 degrees)." Snippet only. The same page reportedly also lists "Interdisciplinary Studies" at 65 degrees, Social Sciences 14 and Liberal Arts & Humanities 22, with internal inconsistencies (gender split given as both 58 men / 97 women and 37.4% / 62.6%). DataUSA warns that CIP categories "may not match the exact concentrations offered by Minerva University". — [DataUSA](https://datausa.io/profile/university/minerva-university)
- No College Scorecard field-of-study earnings for Minerva surfaced on any aggregator. Given the "N/A" institution-level earnings, field-level data almost certainly is not published either. — [CollegeSimply outcomes](https://www.collegesimply.com/colleges/california/minerva-university/outcomes/)

### Inferences
- Minerva's per-college denominators (CS 256, Business 97, AH 104, NS 46, pooled over Classes of 2019–2023 according to Minerva's own majors page) are multi-year totals. IPEDS gives about 155 degrees for the single year 2024. The two cannot be compared directly, and IPEDS cannot confirm the per-college employment rates.
- In IPEDS, Computational Sciences is the largest field (57 of 155 in 2024). That is consistent with Minerva's CS sample being the largest (256).

### Gaps
- No independent source reports employment, graduate-school or earnings outcomes for Arts & Humanities, Business, Computational Sciences, Natural Sciences or Social Sciences separately. Full IPEDS completions by CIP and year (College Navigator) could not be opened.

## 4. Where do independent figures contradict or qualify Minerva's claims?

### Takeaway
No independent source **contradicts** the 91% figure or the per-college placement rates, and none can confirm them. They are self-reported survey results with no federal counterpart. What independent sources do **qualify** is completion and stability. Federal graduation rates (88.8% most recently; earlier or other figures of 79–83%) mean the "91% of graduates" figure covers only students who finished, roughly 11–20% of entrants are not counted, depending on cohort. And WSCUC's 2026 action kept Minerva accredited while flagging finances and cost of attendance for follow-up.

### Cited Findings
**WSCUC accreditation**
- WSCUC directory: status "Accredited"; "Most Recent Commission Action: February 13, 2026". An older cached WSCUC profile shows "Most Recent Commission Action: June 25, 2021" (Seeking Accreditation Visit 1, the initial grant). Snippet only. — [WSCUC institution page](https://wscuc.org/institutions/minerva-university); [WSCUC Feb 2026 actions](https://www.wscuc.org/post/february-2026-commission-actions)
- Commission action letter dated **February 27, 2026**, addressed to "Dr. Mike Magee, President, Minerva University", after a **Special Visit**. It is hosted on Minerva's CDN but is a WSCUC document. Per the search summary, the special visit team's report focused on five required areas, "including the financial model, cost of attendance, and communication and culture". The letter commends Minerva for "Improving transparency and predictability of cost of attendance for students", calls for a stronger financial position, and schedules reaffirmation with an "Offsite Review in fall 2027 and the Accreditation Visit in spring 2028". **Snippet only; I could not open the PDF (host blocked).** The snippet does not show whether the letter discusses graduate outcomes or career data. — [WSCUC Commission Action Letter, Feb 27 2026 (PDF)](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/69b426e177b6b85d9e85edf1_CAL_260227_MINERVA_SV.pdf)
- WSCUC's January 2025 actions list Minerva among institutions whose Interim Report the Commission received. Snippet only. — [WSCUC Jan 2025 actions](https://www.wscuc.org/?p=174384)
- Date conflict: defundminerva.org (advocacy) dates the special visit to December 2–5, 2025, and says philanthropy still covers about half of operations. Treat with caution: it is a critical advocacy site and the claim is unverified. — [defundminerva.org considering-minerva](https://www.defundminerva.org/considering-minerva/)

**Completion versus the "91% of graduates" figure**
- IPEDS-based 150%-time graduation rate is 88.8% (DataUSA, "2023"). Other cohorts or measures show 83.13% (UnivStats, cohort of 160), 81% (BigFuture) and 79% within 6 years (Varsity Tutors, low reliability). — sources as in section 1.
- THE (2022) reported Minerva itself "claims a graduation rate near 85 per cent". — [THE (staging mirror)](https://d7.stg.timeshighereducation.com/node/722015)

**Survey coverage**
- Minerva's own alumni report (data collected November 2022, Classes 2019–2022) says it covers 96% of alumni. It is listed here only because it is hosted by a third party (AISA, aisa.or.ke), but the content is Minerva's. The search summary also points out an internal oddity: one line reads "91% of graduates have founded or co-founded their current organization & secured start-up funding", which conflicts with "15%" on Minerva's impact page. It is probably a layout or summary error, but it is worth checking in the original. — [Minerva Alumni Success Report, AISA mirror](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Agreement:** Forbes 2021 (88%), Niche (91% at 1 year), AcademicJobs (91%) and Daedalus 2024 all match Minerva's own figures for their periods, because all of them come from Minerva (Niche's source is unconfirmed).
- **Qualification 1, the denominator:** "91% of graduates" leaves out non-completers. With an 88.8% graduation rate, about 81% of an entering first-time, full-time cohort would be both graduated and employed or in graduate school (0.888 × 0.91 ≈ 0.81). This is my arithmetic, not a published figure, and it assumes the two rates apply to the same cohort, which has not been verified.
- **Qualification 2, verification:** No federal earnings data exists, so there is no independent check on employment quality or pay. Minerva's per-college figures (CS 95.6% of 256; NS 85% of 46; Business 94.8% of 97; SS 93%; AH 82% of 104) have no independent counterpart.
- **Qualification 3, institutional stability:** The accreditor's 2026 focus on finances and cost of attendance is an independent signal relevant to prospective students. It is not a contradiction of the outcome numbers.
- **Definition drift:** Minerva's own wording moves between "employed full-time or in graduate school", "working or in graduate school" and "full-time roles or graduate school". Forbes 2021 used "full-time professional jobs or ... highly respected graduate programs". These are not identical measures.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full text of the WSCUC February 27, 2026 letter (or the 2021 initial-accreditation letter) to see whether WSCUC commented on student-outcome data, career-outcome methodology or graduation rates. cdn.prod.website-files.com and wscuc.org documents need to be reachable.
- No independent audit or replication of Minerva's six-month placement survey was found.
- I could not establish IPEDS cohort years definitively, so I could not match each graduation-rate figure to a specific entering class.
