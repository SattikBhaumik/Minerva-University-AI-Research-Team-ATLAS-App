# Minerva-authored reports, brochures and PDFs that state undergraduate graduate-outcome numbers

Research date: 2026-11-21. Scope: Minerva-authored documents only (PDFs, brochures, accreditation filings), undergraduate only.

**Access note (read first).** In this session only `minerva.edu` hosts could be downloaded. Minerva hosts nearly all of its PDFs on its Webflow CDN (`cdn.prod.website-files.com`, `assets.website-files.com`, `assets-global.website-files.com`). Those hosts, and `aisa.or.ke`, `amacad.org`, `direct.mit.edu`, `wscuc.org` and `defundminerva.org`, were refused by the network proxy (curl: "CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403"; WebFetch: "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND"). So only one PDF below was read in full ("Deciding to Enroll"). Every other PDF figure is **snippet only**: it comes from search-engine extracts, which can be garbled. To verify them, allow `cdn.prod.website-files.com` and `aisa.or.ke` and re-read the PDFs. The live minerva.edu HTML pages, which were downloaded and parsed, are cited only to compare their numbers with the PDFs.

## Does Minerva publish a full outcomes or "first destination" report with methodology (knowledge rate, response rate, definition of "employed")? What do its numbers say per college?

### Takeaway
No. Searches found no full first-destination report (NACE-style) from Minerva. The only outcomes document is a short marketing PDF, the "Professional Ventures / Alumni Success" report. Its only methodology note: data was collected in November 2022 for the classes of 2019-2022, and it "reflects 96% of alumni". It gives no definition of "employed", no time-to-outcome window, no survey response rate, no sample size and no breakdown by college.

### Cited Findings
- The Alumni Success / Professional Ventures PDF states: "Data was collected in November of 2022 for our graduating classes of 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022. The data reflects 96% of alumni." (snippet only) — [Professional Ventures PDF, Aug 2023](https://assets.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/64e47abf7262a7914c11178c_M24_ProfessionalVentures_8222023.pdf); same wording in the [AISA-hosted "Minerva Alumni Success Report"](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)
- Headline figures in that PDF (snippet only; search extracts show the numbers next to these labels): "91%" of alumni "in full time positions or in graduate programs"; "97%" of alumni "held an internship role prior to graduation"; "15%" "continuing their education" — [Professional Ventures PDF](https://assets.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/64e47abf7262a7914c11178c_M24_ProfessionalVentures_8222023.pdf); [AISA copy](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)
- Also in that PDF (snippet only): "47% of alumni are working in countries different from their home countries"; one extract also gives "70%" of alumni as "not originally from the United States" — [AISA copy](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)
- The PDF has sections headed "Select Graduate Schools", "Select Non-Profits", select companies and achievements. The names in them could not be read (snippet only) — [Professional Ventures PDF](https://assets.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/64e47abf7262a7914c11178c_M24_ProfessionalVentures_8222023.pdf)
- One search extract of the AISA copy puts "91%" beside the label "graduates have founded or co-founded their current organization & secured start-up funding". minerva.edu pairs that same label with 12% ("12% of graduates have founded or co-founded their current organization & secured start-up funding"). The "91%" pairing is most likely a text-extraction error that attached the wrong number to the label (see Inferences) — [AISA copy](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf); [minerva.edu/impact-outcomes-old](https://www.minerva.edu/impact-outcomes-old)
- No per-college outcome breakdown (Arts & Humanities, Business, Computational Sciences, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences) appeared in any Minerva document or snippet found.
- The "Deciding to Enroll" PDF (Dec 2019, read in full) predates the first graduating class's outcomes and has **no graduate-outcome statistics**. Its only percentages about careers are internship-manager survey figures: "90% of managers said Minerva students performed well above average when compared to other interns" and "100% of managers said they would hire Minerva interns again or recommend to others". It gives no sample size or year for that survey. It also says "a large majority of Minerva students secured meaningful roles with top organizations", and that the network includes "companies such as McKinsey & Company, Goldman Sachs, Google, and Twitter, as well as top graduate schools such as KGI, UCSF and Harvard Medical School". Other numbers in it are "With a 2% acceptance rate" and an "80% of the jobs students will fill in the future do not exist today" line attributed to "2014 Study by Siemens USA" — [Deciding to Enroll PDF](https://www.minerva.edu/public/media/enrollment-center/Minerva-Deciding-to-Enroll.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "91% founded…" pairing is almost certainly an extraction artifact. Minerva's own pages, which use the same four-stat block, consistently pair 91% with work or graduate school and 12% with founding.
- "Reflects 96% of alumni" reads as a knowledge rate (outcomes known for 96% of graduates). The PDF does not say whether these came from a survey, LinkedIn or staff records, and gives no headcount. The denominator therefore cannot be reconstructed.
- The current minerva.edu wording ("within six months") adds a time window that the Nov 2022 PDF snippets do not show. The PDF measured status at a single point (Nov 2022) for classes that graduated 0.5 to 3.5 years earlier.

### Gaps
- Full text of the Professional Ventures PDFs (Aug 2023 and Oct 2023 versions) and the AISA copy could not be opened (CDN and aisa.or.ke blocked). The lists of employers, graduate schools and sectors, and any per-class or per-college splits, are unverified.
- The "Minerva Undergraduate Overview" one-pager (Oct 2023) could not be opened. It may repeat the 91/97/12/15 block.

## What does the "Minerva Alumni Success Report" contain, and how current is it?

### Takeaway
It is the same document as Minerva's "Professional Ventures / undergraduate // bachelor's programs / Alumni Success" PDF. Its data was collected in November 2022 and covers the classes of 2019-2022. Minerva's file versions are dated 22 Aug 2023 and 19 Oct 2023, and the Oct 2023 file is the one still linked from minerva.edu/resources. It is now about four years stale: it predates the classes of 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.

### Cited Findings
- The search engine indexes the PDF under the title "Professional Ventures undergraduate // bachelor's programs Alumni Success". The file name `M24_ProfessionalVentures_8222023.pdf` carries the date 8/22/2023 — [Professional Ventures PDF](https://assets.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/64e47abf7262a7914c11178c_M24_ProfessionalVentures_8222023.pdf)
- The AISA copy has the same "Data was collected in November of 2022 for our graduating classes of 2019, 2020, 2021, and 2022" and "96% of alumni" statements (snippet only) — [AISA copy](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)
- minerva.edu/resources still links "Alumni Professional Ventures" to `M24_ProfessionalVentures_10192023_Final_WEB.pdf` (dated 10/19/2023 by file name), as seen in the page HTML on 2026-11-21 — [minerva.edu/resources](https://www.minerva.edu/resources)
- Contents per snippets: 91% full-time or graduate programs; 97% internship before graduation; 15% continuing education; 47% working outside their home country; sections listing select graduate schools, companies, non-profits and achievements (snippet only) — [AISA copy](https://aisa.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/aisa-collaborators/minerva-university-83186/Minerva%20Alumni%20Success%20Report.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "M24" prefix likely marks marketing collateral for the admissions cycle of the class entering around 2024. This is unverified.
- The 91% / 97% / 15% (and 12%) figures on today's minerva.edu Student Impact page match this Nov 2022 dataset number for number. Nothing found shows that the web figures were recomputed for later classes, so the live page most likely still shows the classes of 2019-2022 data without a date.

### Gaps
- Whether the Oct 2023 "Final_WEB" version changed any numbers from the Aug 2023 version is unknown (neither file could be opened).
- No later Alumni Success / Professional Ventures edition (for example 2024 or 2025 data) was found.

## Do Minerva's WSCUC accreditation documents or annual reports give placement or graduation numbers that differ from marketing pages?

### Takeaway
Minerva publishes a "Completion and Retention Rates - November 2025" PDF and posts its WSCUC special-visit team report (Jan 2026) and action letter (Feb 27, 2026). None could be opened. The snippets that were found show completion and retention figures only, no placement figures. The official completion figures could be cited only through an advocacy-site mirror (unverified). No Minerva annual or impact report was found.

### Cited Findings
- minerva.edu/resources: "Minerva University reports annual statistics on student retention and completion. You can view a summary here." The link goes to "Completion and Retention Rates - November 2025" (PDF). Its HTML subpage /resources/student-retention-and-graduation-rates rendered no figures — [minerva.edu/resources](https://www.minerva.edu/resources)
- Accreditation page: "Initial Accreditation Granted… June 2021". Also: "February 2026 Following a special site visit during the winter of 2026, WSCUC affirmed Minerva University's accreditation. The official action letter was published in March 2026." Listed documents: "Special Visit Team Report (December 2025)", "WSCUC Action Letter (February 2026)" — [minerva.edu/accreditation](https://www.minerva.edu/accreditation)
- Action letter (snippet only): addressed "February 27, 2026 Dr. Mike Magee President Minerva University". Per a search summary, the team report focused on "(1) financial model; (2) institutional communication and organizational culture; (3) cost of attendance; (4) library resources; and (5) natural science curriculum and laboratory experiences", and the next full visit is spring 2028. No placement or graduation figure appeared in the snippet — [WSCUC action letter PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/64ca995f0fd30a33b2fd01cc/69b426e177b6b85d9e85edf1_CAL_260227_MINERVA_SV.pdf)
- Completion PDF figures, **third-party mirror only, unverified**: an advocacy site that says it reproduces Minerva's Nov 2025 PDF gives "Class of 2025 at 85.0% completion within 150% of normal time and 98.3% returning for a second year", an all-cohort completion of "83.76%", and the founding class at "75%" — [defundminerva.org/ask](https://www.defundminerva.org/ask/) (advocacy site; could not open; snippet only). Another researcher covers third-party sources; these are listed here only to show that the Minerva PDF exists and roughly what it contains.
- The *Daedalus* essay by Minerva's Cannon and Kosslyn (Spring 2024), snippet only: of the first four graduating classes, "15 percent have been admitted to prestigious graduate and professional schools; another 15 percent started their own ventures" — [Daedalus PDF](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/Daedalus_Sp24_22_Cannon%20&%20Kosslyn.pdf). This came from a search summary and should be checked against the PDF.

### Inferences
- If the Daedalus snippet is accurate, its "another 15 percent started their own ventures" differs from the Alumni Success / web figure of 12% who "founded or co-founded their current organization & secured start-up funding". The two may simply define it differently (any venture vs funded current organization).
- If the mirrored completion figures are accurate, the "Completion and Retention Rates" PDF's all-cohort 83.76% sits below the 88-90% figures quoted elsewhere. Those come from KGI's "approaching 90% within five years" quote and federal-data aggregators, which another researcher covers.

### Gaps
- The text of the Special Visit Team Report (Dec 2025 visit, report dated 2026-01-02) could not be read. It is the document most likely to include independent graduation, retention or outcomes data, and it needs a re-read once the CDN is allowed.
- No WSCUC institutional self-study, interim report (the WSCUC profile reportedly lists a Fall 2024 Interim Report) or annual report authored by Minerva was found online.
- "Evidence of Student Learning at Minerva University 2025" could not be opened. Its title suggests learning-outcome assessment rather than placement.

## Which of these documents is newest, and which numbers are superseded?

### Takeaway
The newest Minerva documents with institutional data are the WSCUC action letter (Feb 27, 2026), the Special Visit Team Report (Jan 2, 2026) and "Completion and Retention Rates - November 2025". These cover completion and retention, not placement. The newest document with **placement** numbers is still the Alumni Success / Professional Ventures PDF (Oct 2023 file, Nov 2022 data, classes 2019-2022). The undated 91% / 97% / 12% / 15% block on minerva.edu matches it and has not been visibly superseded. "Deciding to Enroll" (Dec 2019) is superseded entirely; it predates any graduate outcomes.

### Cited Findings
- Live minerva.edu Student Impact page (downloaded 2026-11-21), for comparison: "91% of graduates are working or in graduate school within six months"; "97% completed at least one internship before graduation"; "12% have founded or co-founded an organization"; "15% pursue graduate study or academic fellowships". It names "Google, McKinsey, DeepMind, and the World Bank" and lists the "Rhodes Scholarship, Schwarzman Scholars, Diana Award, Forbes 30 Under 30, Goldwater Scholarship" — [minerva.edu/student-impact](https://www.minerva.edu/student-impact)
- Earlier minerva.edu wording of the same block: "91% of graduates are working in meaningful roles or pursuing advanced degrees within six months"; "97% completed an internship before graduation"; "12% of graduates have founded or co-founded their current organization & secured start-up funding"; "15% continued with graduate school or academic fellowships"; "2 Rhodes Scholars", Schwarzman Scholars; named: "Brittany Dick (Class of 2024) — Rhodes Scholar… Minerva's second Rhodes Scholar", "Gal Rubin (Class of 2023) — Rhodes Scholar", "Jan Bartkowiak (Class of 2025) — Schwarzman Scholar… Minerva's second Schwarzman Scholar", "Sophia Boler (Class of 2026) — Goldwater Scholar", "Jiayuan Tian (Class of 2025) — Diana Award Recipient"; "More than 100 student-founded ventures and nonprofits are active in the world today" — [minerva.edu/impact-outcomes-old](https://www.minerva.edu/impact-outcomes-old)
- Timeline of documents: Deciding to Enroll (created 2019-12-18) → Professional Ventures (2023-08-22) → Professional Ventures Final_WEB (2023-10-19) → Undergraduate Overview one-pager (2023-10-11) → Evidence of Student Learning (2025) → Completion and Retention Rates (Nov 2025) → Special Visit Team Report (2026-01-02) → WSCUC Action Letter (2026-02-27) — [Deciding to Enroll PDF metadata](https://www.minerva.edu/public/media/enrollment-center/Minerva-Deciding-to-Enroll.pdf); file names linked from [minerva.edu/resources](https://www.minerva.edu/resources) and [minerva.edu/accreditation](https://www.minerva.edu/accreditation)

### Inferences
- The wording has drifted while the numbers stayed the same. "In full time positions or in graduate programs" (PDF) became "working in meaningful roles or pursuing advanced degrees within six months" and then "working or in graduate school within six months". "Founded … & secured start-up funding" became "founded or co-founded an organization". This looks like reworded copy of the Nov 2022 classes 2019-2022 dataset rather than new data. Treat the 91% figure as a classes 2019-2022, Nov 2022 measurement, not a six-month first-destination rate for recent classes.
- The fellowship examples (Class of 2023-2026 winners) are newer than the statistics block, so the page mixes dated anecdotes with undated, older aggregates.

### Gaps
- No Minerva document states placement outcomes for the classes of 2023-2026. Whether such data exists internally (for example in the WSCUC team report) is unknown.
- No document gives an outcome breakdown by college; this remains unanswered.
